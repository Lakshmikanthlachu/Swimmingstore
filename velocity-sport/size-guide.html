Find Your Fit

Every VOLT category is cut differently. Use the charts below, or follow our 3-step measuring guide, to order with confidence — solo or for a full squad.

Three steps

How To Measure Yourself

1

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms and across your shoulder blades. Keep the tape level and snug, not tight.

2

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest point of your torso, usually just above your belly button.

3

Hips

Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and glutes, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
